          How this works

          Runs added. For every ball of IPL 2026 we ask: from this exact match situation (balls left, wickets down), how many runs does an average team score? A batter who beats that expectation adds runs; getting out costs runs. A bowler gets the opposite. Totals are centred so the average player in 2026 adds zero.

          Replacement level. Teams can always sign a minimum-price player (₹30 lakh). We measure how those players actually performed, then count each player's runs above replacement.

          The going rate. We divide what teams spent above the minimum by all the runs above replacement in the league. That gives the price of one run, , and each player's value delivered = ₹30 lakh + their runs above replacement × that rate.

          What it doesn't count. Wicketkeeping, fielding, captaincy, leadership, and what a player adds off the field. Value is one season, so a quiet year after a great one shows up as a quiet year. Second innings use the same expectations as first innings. Players listed as withdrawn or injured are left out.
